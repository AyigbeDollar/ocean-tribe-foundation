## Pay, hours and leave

### 14. How much is minimum wage now

**Example questions:** "how much is minimum wage now", "what is the minimum wage in 2026", "daily minimum wage ghana", "national daily minimum wage", "minimum wage 2025"

**Answer:**

The **National Daily Minimum Wage for 2026 is GH¢21.77** per day, effective **1 January 2026**. In 2025 it was GH¢19.97.

It is set by the National Tripartite Committee (government, employers and organised labour). No employer may pay a worker below it.

### 16. How many days annual leave

**Example questions:** "how many days annual leave", "how many leave days do i get", "am i entitled to annual leave", "holiday leave days"

**Answer:**

Under section 20 of the Labour Act (Act 651), a worker is entitled to **at least 15 working days of paid annual leave** after **12 months of continuous service**.

Your contract or collective agreement may give more. Leave cannot be given up for money, except where the law or agreement allows.

**Buttons:** Workers' rights (`/workers-rights`) | FAQ (`/faq`)

### 17. How many hours should i work

**Example questions:** "how many hours should i work", "maximum working hours", "working hours per week", "my boss makes me work 12 hours"

**Answer:**

The normal maximum is **8 hours a day** and **40 hours a week**. Work beyond these hours is **overtime** and must be paid at the agreed overtime rate.

If you are regularly made to work longer without overtime pay, report it to the Labour Department.

## Dismissal and complaints

### 22. How much notice must my employer give

**Example questions:** "how much notice must my employer give", "notice period for termination", "can i be sacked without notice", "how much notice to resign", "pay in lieu of notice"

**Answer:**

Minimum notice under the Labour Act (either side can give pay instead of notice):

- Contract of **3 years or more**: 1 month&#39;s notice or 1 month&#39;s pay

- Contract **under 3 years**: 2 weeks&#39; notice or 2 weeks&#39; pay

- **Week to week** contract: 7 days&#39; notice

Your contract may give longer notice.

## Child labour

### 25. What is the minimum age to work in ghana

**Example questions:** "what is the minimum age to work in ghana", "can a 14 year old work", "is child labour illegal", "at what age can a child work", "hazardous work age"

**Answer:**

Under the Children&#39;s Act, 1998 (Act 560):

- Minimum age for employment: **15 years**

- Light work (not harmful, does not affect school): from **13 years**

- Hazardous work (mining, fishing at sea, heavy loads, chemicals, night work): **18 years**

- No child may do night work between **8pm and 6am**

## Safety, injury and inspection

### 28. I was injured at work

**Example questions:** "i was injured at work", "workmen's compensation", "accident at work who pays", "compensation for injury at work", "worker died at work"

**Answer:**

Under the **Workmen&#39;s Compensation Law, 1987 (PNDCL 187)**, a worker injured (or the family of a worker killed) in a work accident may be compensated:

- The employer must report the accident to the **Labour Department**

- The worker has a medical assessment of the injury

- The Department assesses the claim and the **employer or its insurer pays**

Get medical care first, then contact your nearest office.

**Buttons:** [Report an accident on GLMIS](https://www.glmis.gov.gh/accidents) | Compensation claims (`/services#compensation`) | Find an office (`/contact`)

A worker may report unsafe conditions to the Labour Department, and should not be punished for doing so.

## Agencies, scams and working abroad

### 33. I want to open a recruitment agency

**Example questions:** "i want to open a recruitment agency", "how to license a private employment agency", "requirements for employment agency licence", "start a manpower agency", "agency quarterly returns"

**Answer:**

To operate a **private employment agency** you need a licence:

- Register your company with the **Registrar-General**

- Apply to the Minister through the **Labour Department**

- Your premises and staff are inspected

- If approved, a licence is issued

- Submit **quarterly returns within 14 days** after each quarter

Operating without a licence is illegal.

**Buttons:** Agency licensing (`/services#agencies`) | Contact us (`/contact`)

### 34. Is this job abroad real

**Example questions:** "is this job abroad real", "an agent wants money for a visa job", "how do i know if an agency is licensed", "fake recruitment agency", "job scam", "visa lottery job"

**Answer:**

**Be careful.** Warning signs of a recruitment scam:

- You must pay money for a &quot;visa lottery&quot;, processing or a guaranteed job

- No written contract, or the agency has no licence

- Pressure to travel quickly or hand over your passport

Only use **licensed** agencies and verify them with the Labour Department before paying or travelling. Genuine local jobs are on GLMIS for free.
