## What is GLMIS?

Looking for work online can feel confusing. There are many websites, and it is hard to know which ones to trust. This guide shows you one you can trust.

The Ghana Labour Market Information System (GLMIS) is a free national platform. It links job seekers, employers, training providers and policymakers in one place. Policymakers are the people who make decisions about jobs and skills in Ghana.

The Ghana Labour Department helps job seekers use GLMIS. For you, it is a place to show your skills and search for jobs. It costs nothing to use.

## What you need before you start

You'll need your Ghana Card and a phone number. It also helps to have:

-   a phone or computer with internet
-   a CV, if you have one (a short document about your education and work)
-   a list of your certificates and past jobs

Do not worry if you do not have a CV yet. You can still create a profile. Staff at a Public Employment Centre (PEC) can help you write one.

## How to register: step by step

1.  Go to [glmis.gov.gh](https://glmis.gov.gh) on your phone or computer.
2.  Choose the option for job seekers.
3.  Sign up with your details and your phone number. Keep your login details safe.
4.  Complete your profile. Add your qualifications, skills, work experience and the jobs you would like.
5.  Upload your CV if you have one.
6.  Search for jobs that match your skills, and apply.
7.  Keep your profile up to date. Add new skills, training or jobs as you get them.

The website may change over time. If a step looks different, follow the instructions on the screen. You can also ask us for help.

## Tips for a strong profile

Employers read many profiles. A clear and complete profile helps yours stand out.

-   **Fill in every section.** Empty sections make it harder to match you with jobs.
-   **Be honest.** List only the qualifications and experience you really have.
-   **Include informal work.** Helping in a family shop or on a farm still counts as experience.
-   **Name your skills clearly.** For example, write "tiling", "sewing" or "driving".
-   **Check your phone number.** Employers may use it to contact you.
-   **Log in often.** New jobs are added, so check back regularly.

> **Remember:** GLMIS is free. The Department never charges for jobs. If anyone asks you to pay to register or to get a job, do not pay. Read our guide on how to [spot a recruitment scam](/blog/spot-recruitment-scams).

## Can't go online? Visit a PEC

Not everyone has a smartphone or a computer. That is fine. You can still use GLMIS.

Visit your nearest PEC. Our staff will help you create your profile and search for jobs. Bring your Ghana Card and a phone number. Find your nearest centre in our [PEC directory](/contact).

PECs can also give you career guidance and help with your CV. Read about the [five ways your PEC can help](/blog/public-employment-centre-services).

## For employers: post your vacancies free

Are you an employer looking for staff? You can post vacancies on GLMIS free of charge.

Job seekers across Ghana can then find your vacancy and apply. Our PEC staff can also help match your vacancy with suitable candidates.

Posting on GLMIS also helps the country. It gives policymakers better information about which skills employers need. That helps training providers plan the right courses.

Visit [glmis.gov.gh](https://glmis.gov.gh) to get started, or contact your nearest PEC for help.

## Take the first step today

Registering takes a little time, but it puts your skills in front of employers. We cannot promise you a job. But a complete GLMIS profile helps you get ready for the right one.

Go to [glmis.gov.gh](https://glmis.gov.gh) and create your profile today. If you get stuck, we are here to help.
