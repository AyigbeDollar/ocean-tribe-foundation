Your local centre is here for you

Looking for work can feel lonely and tiring. You send messages, you wait, and often nobody replies. You do not have to do it alone.

The Ghana Labour Department runs Public Employment Centres (PECs) across the country. A PEC is a free government office. It helps people find work and stay safe at work.

Here are five ways your nearest PEC can help you.

1. Matching you with real vacancies

Employers tell us about jobs they need to fill. Our staff match those vacancies with job seekers who have the right skills.

Many vacancies are also listed on the Ghana Labour Market Information System (GLMIS). GLMIS is a free national website for jobs and labour information. You can visit glmis.gov.gh to search for jobs yourself.

The vacancies we share come from employers we work with. This lowers the risk of fake adverts. We cannot promise you a job, but we can help you find real openings.

2. Career guidance and counselling

Not sure what work suits you? That is normal. Our officers can sit with you and talk about your skills, interests and experience.

Career guidance means advice on the kind of work you could do. Counselling means support when you feel stuck or unsure. We can help you:

  • understand which jobs match your skills
  • write or improve your CV (a short document about your work and education)
  • prepare for job interviews
  • find training that could open new doors

Young people leaving school are welcome. So are adults who want to change careers.

3. Help to get online

More and more jobs are posted online. You may not have a computer. You may not feel confident on the internet. We can help.

Staff at your PEC can help you create a GLMIS profile. A profile is your page on GLMIS. It shows employers your skills and experience. Here is what usually happens:

  1. Visit your nearest PEC with your Ghana Card and a phone number.
  2. Tell a staff member you want to register on GLMIS.
  3. They guide you to create your profile, step by step.
  4. You add your education, skills, experience and the jobs you want.
  5. You leave knowing how to log in and search for jobs.

You can also read our full guide on how to register on GLMIS.

4. Protecting you at work

Finding a job is only the start. You also deserve fair treatment once you are hired.

Our Labour Officers carry out labour inspections. An inspection is a visit to a workplace. The officer checks that the employer follows the law on things like wages, working hours and safety.

You may have a problem with your employer. If so, you can bring a complaint to us. We offer free mediation. Mediation means we sit with you and your employer to find a fair solution together.

Find out how on our complaints page. You can also read more about your rights at work.

5. Support if you are injured at work

Accidents can happen, even in safe workplaces. If you are hurt at work, you may be able to claim workmen's compensation. This means payment when you are hurt at work.

Our officers can explain how a claim works. They can help you fill in the forms and follow your case. Every case is different, so we cannot promise a payment. But we will guide you through each step.

Learn more on our workmen's compensation page.

Remember: The Department never charges for jobs. If anyone asks you to pay for a job in our name, do not pay. Call us toll-free on 0800 600 300.

How to visit your nearest PEC

Find the PEC closest to you in our PEC directory. You do not need to know anyone there. Just walk in and tell us how we can help.

Bring your Ghana Card. If you have a CV or certificates, bring copies of those too. All our services are free.

Ready to start? Search for jobs on GLMIS today, or visit your nearest PEC. We are here to help you take the next step.
