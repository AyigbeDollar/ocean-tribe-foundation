A plain-language summary of the Labour Act, 2003 (Act 651) for every worker in Ghana.

Coming soon: a new Labour Act

Government is finalising a new Labour Bill to replace Act 651. It is expected to extend protection to gig, platform and remote workers. Until Parliament passes it, the Labour Act, 2003 remains the law. We will update this page when the new law takes effect.

This page is general guidance. The text of the Labour Act, 2003 (Act 651) is the authoritative source.
