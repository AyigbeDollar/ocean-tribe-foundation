Privacy notice

How this website and LD Assist, our automated assistant, handle your information.

Legal basis: Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), overseen by the Data Protection Commission.

What we collect

  • LD Assist conversations: the questions you type are used to answer you. They are kept in your browser for this visit only and are cleared when you close the tab.
  • AI answers: when AI answers are switched on, your recent questions in this chat are sent securely to our AI service provider to write a reply. They are not used to train AI models.
  • Usage data: anonymous counts of page visits, button clicks and shares help us improve the site. We do not collect your name, phone number or Ghana Card number through this website.

What you should never share in the chat

Never type your Ghana Card number, bank or mobile money details, PINs or passwords. Our officers will never ask for these in a chat.

Personal cases

If you need help with a personal case, such as a dismissal, unpaid wages or an injury at work, please call us free on 0800 600 300 or visit your nearest Public Employment Centre. A Labour Officer will ask for your consent before recording your details.

Links to other sites

This site links to the Ghana Labour Market Information System (GLMIS) at glmis.gov.gh and to social media platforms. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Contact

For questions about your information, email info@labourdepartment.gov.gh or call 0800 600 300.
