Free job alerts

Get first pick of new jobs and opportunities

Be among the first to hear about new vacancies, job fairs and free training in your region. We send them to your WhatsApp or email. It is free, and you can stop at any time.

What you will get

Short, useful updates. Nothing to pay, ever.

New vacancies

Fresh jobs from the Ghana Labour Market Information System (GLMIS) and our Public Employment Centres (PECs), with a direct link to apply.

Job fairs near you

Recruitment drives, open days and the JOBS (Just One Bold Step) roadshow when it comes to your region.

Free training

Skills programmes, apprenticeships and internships, including national programmes for young people.

Scam alerts

Warnings about fake recruiters and visa scams. Remember: the Department never charges for jobs.

Your details are safe. We use them only to send job and opportunity updates from the Ghana Labour Department, in line with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). We never sell or share them. To stop, reply STOP on WhatsApp or use the unsubscribe link in any email. Read our privacy notice.
