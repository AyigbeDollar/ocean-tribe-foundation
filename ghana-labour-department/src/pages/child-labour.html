How to report child labour

If you see a child doing harmful work, out of school to work, or working at night, report it.

  • Your nearest Labour Department office (Child Labour Unit)
  • The District Assembly or Department of Social Welfare
  • The Ghana Police Service on 191 or 18555 if a child is in immediate danger

You can report confidentially. Give the location, what you saw and when.

The Child Labour Unit

The Child Labour Unit (CLU) sits within the Ghana Labour Department. It is the national secretariat of the National Steering Committee on Child Labour.

  • Coordinates all child labour interventions across Ghana
  • Runs the Ghana Child Labour Monitoring System (GCLMS) with District Assemblies and communities
  • Leads the Ghana Accelerated Action Plan Against Child Labour 2023 to 2027
  • Works with Social Welfare, the Police, the cocoa and mining sectors, and partners such as the ILO and UNICEF

What counts as hazardous work?

Work that puts a child's health, safety or morals at risk is hazardous. Examples include mining and quarrying, carrying heavy loads, fishing at sea, work with chemicals or sharp tools, work in bars and hotels, and work at night. Light work is only allowed if it does not harm the child's health or interfere with school.

Children may help at home or on the family farm in ways that are safe, age-appropriate and do not affect schooling. That is not child labour.
