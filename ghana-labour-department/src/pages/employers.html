Your key obligations

Written terms

Give every worker employed for 6 months or more a written statement of their terms within 2 months of starting.

Fair pay

Pay at least the national daily minimum wage (GH¢{{minWage}} in 2026), equal pay for equal work, and pay on time.

Hours and leave

Respect the 40-hour week, grant at least 15 days' annual leave and 12 weeks' paid maternity leave.

Health and safety

Provide safe premises, protective equipment and training, and report workplace accidents to the Labour Department.

Records

Keep records of workers, wages, hours and leave, and make them available to Labour Inspectors.

No child labour

Do not employ anyone under 15, or anyone under 18 in hazardous work or at night.

Using a recruitment agency?

Only work with private employment agencies licensed by the Labour Department. Unlicensed recruitment is an offence under section 7 of the Labour Act. See licensing requirements.
