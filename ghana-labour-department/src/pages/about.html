Our mandate

The Labour Department is mandated to ensure employment intermediation, promotion and maintain international relations to ensure harmonious industrial relations in the country as guided by the 1992 Constitution of Ghana, the Labour Act, 2003 (Act 651), and other relevant enactments.

Established on 1 April 1938, the Department is one of the civil service departments under the Ministry of Labour, Jobs and Employment. Through our head office in Accra and Public Employment Centres (PECs) in all 16 regions, we help job seekers find work, guide employers on their duties and settle workplace disputes before they become conflicts.

We administer the Labour Act, 2003 (Act 651), the Labour Regulations, 2007 (L.I. 1833) and the Workmen's Compensation Law, 1987 (PNDCL 187), and work with partners to enforce the child labour provisions of the Children's Act, 1998 (Act 560).

Vision

“A one-stop shop state-of-the-art centre of excellence for Labour Market Information and employer/employee protection.”

Mission

The Labour Department exists to create and maintain a favourable employment environment through employment service delivery, labour inspections, promotion of harmonious industrial relations, health and safety, international relations and elimination of child labour for national development in a well-resourced environment with motivated staff.

Our units and services

Nine areas of work, one goal: decent work for every worker and fair dealing for every employer.

National Employment Service

Free registration, job matching and placement through Public Employment Centres and GLMIS, and canvassing employers for vacancies.

Vocational guidance and counselling

Career advice that helps job seekers match their skills to work and plan training.

Labour inspection

Routine and complaint-based workplace inspections on wages, hours, leave, records and safety.

Industrial relations and disputes

Registering trade unions and employers' associations, issuing collective bargaining certificates and mediating individual complaints for free.

Child Labour Unit

The national secretariat of the National Steering Committee on Child Labour. It coordinates all child labour interventions and runs the Ghana Child Labour Monitoring System. Learn more about child labour

Private Employment Agencies

Licensing and monitoring Private Employment Agencies (PEAs), including those recruiting Ghanaians for work abroad.

Workmen's compensation

Handling claims for workmen's compensation (payment when you are hurt or made ill at work).

Labour market information

Collecting and publishing labour statistics and GLMIS data that guide policy, training and investment.

International labour affairs

Representing Ghana in international labour matters and promoting fair recruitment and safe labour migration.
