Our mandate

The Labour Department is mandated to ensure employment intermediation, promotion and maintain international relations to ensure harmonious industrial relations in the country as guided by the 1992 Constitution of Ghana, the Labour Act, 2003 (Act 651), and other relevant enactments.

We are one of the civil service departments under the Ministry of Labour, Jobs and Employment, with Public Employment Centres (PECs) in all 16 regions.

Vision

“A one-stop shop state-of-the-art centre of excellence for Labour Market Information and employer/employee protection.”

Mission

The Labour Department exists to create and maintain a favourable employment environment through employment service delivery, labour inspections, promotion of harmonious industrial relations, health and safety, international relations and elimination of child labour for national development in a well-resourced environment with motivated staff.

What we do

National Employment Service

Registering and placing job seekers, canvassing for jobs, and vocational guidance and counselling through PECs and GLMIS.

Labour inspection

Checking workplaces for fair wages, hours, leave, records, and health and safety.

Industrial relations

Registering trade unions and employers' associations, issuing collective bargaining certificates and resolving disputes.

Private Employment Agencies

Registering and monitoring Private Employment Agencies (PEAs), including those recruiting for work abroad.

Workmen's compensation

Handling claims for workmen's compensation (payment when you are hurt at work).

Child labour and international affairs

Child labour monitoring with partners, and international labour affairs.

Management

Leadership

Braimah Ibrahim Dawuda

Chief Labour Officer

As Chief Labour Officer, Mr Dawuda leads the Ghana Labour Department and its nationwide network of district labour offices and Public Employment Centres. He has championed the expansion of free public employment services, including the nationwide JOBS (Just One Bold Step) roadshow and the rollout of the Ghana Labour Market Information System.

Under his leadership the Department is modernising labour administration, strengthening workplace inspection and bringing job services closer to young people in every region.
