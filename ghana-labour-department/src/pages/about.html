Protecting workers and connecting Ghana to work since 1938

We are the government agency responsible for labour administration, employment services and fair, safe workplaces across Ghana.

Who we are

The Labour Department was established on 1 April 1938 and today operates under the Ministry of Labour, Jobs and Employment. We administer the Labour Act, 2003 (Act 651), the Labour Regulations, 2007 (L.I. 1833) and the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1987 (PNDCL 187).

Through our head office in Accra and Public Employment Centres in all 16 regions, we help job seekers find work, guide employers on their duties and settle workplace disputes before they become conflicts.

Vision

To become a one-stop Labour Market Information provider, a reliable partner in matching labour supply with demand and a protector of workers' rights.

Mission

To coordinate employment opportunities and labour interventions in all sectors, promote decent jobs and ensure harmonious labour relations.

What we do

Employment services

Free job matching, career counselling and registration through Public Employment Centres and GLMIS.

Labour inspection

Workplace inspections to check compliance with hours, wages, leave and safety standards.

Dispute settlement

Free mediation of individual complaints such as unpaid wages and unfair dismissal.

Agency licensing

Licensing and monitoring of private employment agencies, including those recruiting for jobs abroad.

Child labour

Our Child Labour Unit is the national secretariat coordinating action against child labour.

Labour market data

Collecting and publishing labour statistics that guide policy, training and investment.

Management

Leadership

Braimah Ibrahim Dawuda

Chief Labour Officer

As Chief Labour Officer, Mr Dawuda leads the Labour Department and its nationwide network of Labour Offices and Public Employment Centres. He has championed the expansion of free public employment services, including the nationwide JOBS (Just One Bold Step) roadshow and the rollout of the Ghana Labour Market Information System.

Under his leadership the Department is modernising labour administration, strengthening workplace inspection and bringing job services closer to young people in every region.
