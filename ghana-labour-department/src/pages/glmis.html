One free profile connects you to employers and vacancies across all 16 regions.

Go to glmis.gov.gh

Register in four steps

It takes about 15 minutes and costs nothing.

  1. 1

    Open GLMIS

    Visit glmis.gov.gh on your phone or computer.

  2. 2

    Create an account

    Choose "job seeker" and sign up with your details.

  3. 3

    Build your profile

    Add qualifications, skills, experience and preferred jobs. Upload your CV.

  4. 4

    Get matched

    Browse and apply for vacancies, and let employers find you.

Who GLMIS is for

Job seekers

Graduates, artisans, experienced professionals and anyone looking for work or a better job.

Employers

Businesses and institutions posting vacancies and searching for candidates for free.

Policy and research

Planners using labour market information on skills, demand and trends.

Training providers

Schools and TVET institutions aligning courses with what employers need.

Is GLMIS free?
Yes. Registering, creating a profile, applying for jobs and posting vacancies are all free. Never pay anyone to register you.
I do not have internet access or need help. What can I do?
Visit any Labour Department office or Public Employment Centre. Staff will register you on the spot and help you build your profile.
Does GLMIS guarantee me a job?
No platform can guarantee a job, but a complete, up-to-date profile greatly improves your chances of being matched. Update your CV and skills regularly.
How do employers post a vacancy?
Sign up as an employer on glmis.gov.gh, create your company profile and post the vacancy. Your regional Public Employment Centre can also help you shortlist candidates.
