How we resolve a workplace complaint

Most disputes are settled at the first meeting. Here is what to expect.

  1. 1

    Raise it at work

    Speak to your supervisor or HR first and keep copies of contracts, payslips and messages.

  2. 2

    Visit a labour office

    Bring your documents to the nearest Labour Department office or Public Employment Centre.

  3. 3

    Free mediation

    A Labour Officer invites both sides and helps you reach a fair settlement under the law.

  4. 4

    Agree the outcome

    Most cases end in a written settlement. If no agreement is reached, the Labour Officer advises you on your further options under the law.

Your rights at work, in plain language

Hours and leave

  • Normal hours are 8 a day and 40 a week.
  • At least 15 working days of paid annual leave after 12 months of continuous service.
  • At least 12 weeks of paid maternity leave, plus 2 extra weeks for abnormal or multiple births.

Pay and dismissal

  • No one may be paid below the national daily minimum wage of GH¢{{minWage}} ({{minWageYear}}).
  • Dismissal needs proper notice or pay in lieu, and a fair reason.
  • Dismissal for union membership or pregnancy is unfair under the Labour Act.
